About Me

My journey with music and rock instrumentals

Welcome! My name is Misganaw Tadesse. I simply love music, especially rock music, and I am deeply influenced by the sound of Hillsong during the 2000–2010 era, as well as Ethiopian evangelical church music.

Even though I didn't have formal music education, I believe in being faithful to bring whatever I have to God—just like the five loaves and two fish in the Bible—trusting Him to bless and use it. Ethiopian church music may not be as technically advanced as music in other parts of the world, but we have witnessed God powerfully use faithful musicians to impact countlessly many lives in Ethiopia. Following their example, I try to do something with what I have and continue in it. Recognizing that my current musical knowledge is limited, I have a deep desire to receive formal education in music theory, arrangement, performance, recording, and mixing knowledge under structured academic guidance.

Scholarship & Academic Portfolio

Desire for formal growth and skill development

Operating primarily in Cubase 5, I record my lead electric guitars live, incorporate live bass on select tracks, and build foundational rhythms using VST virtual instruments for drums and bass. I listen closely to existing rock tracks to recreate and imitate their sound by ear, while consulting learning resources and AI guidance to support my mixing process.
