About Me

Where my music comes from

I'm Misganaw Tadesse, a guitarist and self-taught producer from Ethiopia. I write and record rock music, and my sound is shaped by Hillsong's music from 2000 to 2010 and by Ethiopian evangelical church music.

I have no formal music education. I bring what I have to God, like the five loaves and two fish in the Bible, and trust Him to use it. Ethiopian church music taught me that faithfulness matters as much as technique. I have seen God use faithful musicians to touch countless lives in Ethiopia, and I want to follow their example.

I now want to add technical skill to that foundation, through structured study of music theory, arrangement, performance, recording and mixing.

My Process & Skills

How I make music today

I record my lead electric guitars live, add live bass on select tracks, and build the foundation with virtual drum and bass instruments (VSTs). I learn by listening closely to professional tracks and recreating their sound by ear, and I use learning resources and AI guidance to support my mixing. This works, but I have reached the point where I need a teacher to show me what I can't yet see on my own.

What I Want to Study

The training I'm looking for, and why

I am looking for formal, mentored training in contemporary music. These are the skills I want to build:

  • Music theory and arrangement: to write and arrange with confidence instead of relying only on my ear.
  • Performance: to grow as a guitarist with expert feedback.
  • Recording and mixing: to reach professional sound quality in my own productions.

I want to use this training to serve the church, and to strengthen the music I make for Ethiopian congregations.
